StudyBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

StudyBuddy is a student productivity application that helps users organize study schedules and manage study reminders. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

2. Information We Handle

  • Email address used for account registration and login.
  • Profile photos selected using your camera or gallery.
  • Study reminder titles, subjects, dates and times.

3. Data Storage

Firebase Authentication is used to manage user accounts. Study reminders and profile photo information are stored locally on your device using local storage.

4. Camera and Photo Library

Camera and photo library permissions are requested when you choose to take or select a profile picture. You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

5. Notifications

StudyBuddy schedules local notifications to remind you about your study sessions. Notification permissions can be managed through your device settings.
